# Covie brand direction

Covie Brand Bible v2.0 (22 September 2026) is authoritative. This repository guide summarises its multi-calendar identity; template manifests and canonical controls implement it.

## Brand idea

Covie is a shared-calendar ecosystem, with Personal, Social Groups, Shared Facilities, Salon Bookings, Staff Rosters and Co-parenting. The tone is **bright calm**: optimistic, organised, warm and dependable.

Covie is for everyday coordination between people, groups and businesses. It should not look legal, clinical, corporate or childish.

## Solid-colour rule

Covie branding and marketing surfaces use **solid colours only**.

Do not introduce:
- gradients or mesh gradients
- glow effects or coloured blurs
- glassmorphism or translucent colour washes
- decorative floating SaaS cards
- generic purple/blue AI-startup treatments

Half-day ownership inside the functional calendar may continue to use its existing hard 50/50 split rendering because it communicates two distinct parent states rather than a decorative colour gradient.

## Core palette

- Coral: `#FF6B5F` — primary brand action and warmth
- Teal: `#19A897` — secondary brand colour and schedule identity
- Sunshine: `#F4C64E` — highlights and important moments
- Violet: `#765ED6` — agreements and secondary parent/accent
- Ink: `#243139` — primary text and strong outlines
- Warm cream: `#FFF9F2` — primary marketing background

Use Ink text on Coral, Teal and Sunshine. Use white text on Violet. This keeps normal body copy at accessible contrast.

## Logo

The Covie Loop mark is made from two solid coloured halves that form one continuous loop.

Meaning:
- people and shared commitments
- connected calendars
- one dependable coordination system

Do not replace the mark with a house, heart, family silhouette or calendar icon.

## Typography

- Display hierarchy: Fraunces
- Product and body UI: Geist

Use Fraunces for page and dialog titles. Use Geist for interface labels, records and operational content. Preserve the canonical controls, minimum 44px independent targets and visible focus.

## Marketing composition

Prefer:
- asymmetric layouts
- strong full-width colour chapters
- real product states shown at useful scale
- editorial typography
- modest corner radii
- strong outlines and solid offset shadows where appropriate

Avoid repeating grids of identical feature cards. Schedule, Expenses, Responsibilities and Agreements should each feel like a chapter with its own visual treatment.

## Photography

When Covie introduces photography, prefer candid everyday family logistics: school bags, sports, pickups, meals, birthday preparation and handovers. Avoid staged "happy divorced family" stock imagery.

Only add photography when we have an appropriate licensed or owned image set.
